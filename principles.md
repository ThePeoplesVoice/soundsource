# Universal Principles for SoundSource

These are fundamental. They do not change with market narrative.

## 1. Sound Value
Income must derive from real economic activity: interest paid by borrowers, fees paid by traders, yield from productive assets, or service revenue. Token emissions, subsidy mining, and pure inflation are filtered. Sustainability score reflects the ratio of real yield to emissions dependence.

## 2. Sourced Transparency
Every number is a ledger row:
`as_of | metric | value | unit | source | locator | confidence | last_checked`

When sources disagree, both rows are kept. Drift is the signal, not the error to hide.

## 3. Incentive Alignment
Scoring rewards systems where providers, users, and long-term participants share in health. Short-term extraction (high emissions, governance capture, fee diversion) lowers the alignment score. Management suggestions penalize misalignment.

## 4. Risk Isolation
Each source declares its failure modes (oracle, liquidation, legal, liquidity, slashing, governance). Exposure is modular. No automatic cascading. User sets risk tolerance; engine applies a penalty above that threshold.

## 5. Optionality & Control
Self-custody remains the default. Any managed layer is optional, fully disclosed, and reversible. The engine produces recommendations, not forced actions. Users retain keys and exit rights.

## 6. Adaptive Resilience
The system measures outcomes, detects drift between expected and observed, and evolves scoring weights from evidence. Rules are not fixed ideology; they are tested against primary sources and live results.

## Integrity Filter (applied to every addition)
1. Is it true?
2. Is it high-signal?
3. Does it help something leave the ground?

Fail any one → defer.
